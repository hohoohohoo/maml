## §A1. Algorithm 1 --- Per-arc turn-on graph extraction

\input{A1_algorithm_extraction.tex}

**Corner cases handled by the same algorithm:**

- Parallel transistors $\to$ multiple DFS paths, all kept in the union.
- Stacked transistors $\to$ a single multi-hop path.
- Reconvergent paths $\to$ naturally merged by the per-stage union.
- Internal nodes $\to$ become stage targets for the next (input-side) stage.
- Input-state dependence $\to$ fixed by the arc's `when` clause, which
  pins non-switching inputs and thus the conducting configuration.
