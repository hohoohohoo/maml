================================================================================
NOTE ON 128 CORNERS (kept by design)
================================================================================
`invbuf_a_b_c` and `simple_a_b_c` describe cells from two different families at
the *same* physical process corner (a, b, c). In this build they are stored as
two independent corner labels (128 total) — this is correct for training and
validation (model behavior is identical to merging them), and any per-corner
analysis can group `invbuf_*` and `simple_*` afterwards if needed.
